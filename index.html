How does the photo estimate process work?

Take 2 to 3 clear photos of the area you want worked on (such as a shower enclosure, kitchen wall, or vanity setup) and text or upload them through our contact form. We will evaluate the scope and issue a dependable baseline quote.

Are you licensed plumbers?

Plumbing Gurus LLC specializes in high-end tile installations, structural framing modifications, and cosmetic home improvements. For specialized water and gas supply lines, we coordinate alongside our licensed Texas plumbing partners to meet strict code requirements.

Can I supply my own tile and fixtures?

Certainly. You are welcome to source your own tile, vanities, and hardware, or we can gladly guide you on quality materials available locally around San Antonio.
