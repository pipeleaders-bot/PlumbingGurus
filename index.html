Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Text us photos for a fast preliminary estimate, or fill out the form on this page.

What areas do you serve?

San Antonio and surrounding communities, including Stone Oak, Helotes, Alamo Ranch, Schertz, Cibolo, Universal City, Live Oak, Converse, and Leon Valley.

Do you do plumbing work like installing toilets or faucets?

No — that work requires a Texas plumbing license. We handle the tile, surround, and carpentry, and coordinate with a licensed plumber for any water or gas line connections.

Who actually connects my new faucet or toilet — you or a plumber?

Our team handles removal, prep, and finish work on-site. The actual connection to your water, drain, or gas lines is performed by our licensed Texas plumbing partner, and priced together as one project.

Do you install customer-supplied vanities or cabinets?

Yes, for the carpentry and fitting portion.

Do you rent out heavy equipment?

Yes. Visit our equipment rental page for available machines, rates, and availability.
